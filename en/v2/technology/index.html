02 BONDING

Bonded direct, no interface layer

The manifold and the microchannel wafer are Si–Si oxide-bonded, and the channel layer is bonded directly to the back of the die in place of TIM and a spreader. No adhesive, no solder interface.

03 FLOW

Even flow to every channel

A mirrored U-path keeps flow and pressure drop equal on both sides, so no region runs short and forms a hot spot. Parallel microchannels greatly increase the solid–liquid contact area.

05 SYSTEM

System requirements

MCL is direct liquid cooling. Unlike air cooling, the system side needs three things:

Pump
Drives the coolant around the loop
Liquid cooling loop
Tubing and fittings carry coolant into MCL and take the heat to a heat exchanger
Leak-proof design
Fittings and tubing for direct liquid cooling need a leak-proof design, planned together with the integration
