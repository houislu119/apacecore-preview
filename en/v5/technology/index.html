01 STRUCTURE

Two bonds with no adhesive or solder

The manifold and the microchannel layer are two wafers bonded together, then bonded as one piece to the back of the die. Heat rises straight into the channels without crossing glue or solder.

Bonds and material

Bond 1
Manifold ↔ microchannels
Si–Si oxide bond
Forms a sealed, adhesive-free channel cavity
Bond 2
Channel layer ↔ back of die
Direct bond
Removes TIM and spreader, the main interface resistance

02 FLOW

A mirrored U-shaped flow path

One inlet, one outlet. Coolant runs down the central trunk, splits into the parallel channels on both sides, merges at the bottom and leaves.

  1. 1

    Inlet

    Coolant enters through the inlet in the manifold

  2. 2

    Central split

    It runs down the central trunk and splits to both sides

  3. 3

    Parallel channels

    It crosses the parallel channels, picking up heat and turning from blue to amber

  4. 4

    Merge and exit

    The flow merges at the bottom and leaves through the outlet

Why this layout

  • Mirrored sides keep flow and pressure drop equal, so no region runs short and forms a hot spot
  • Parallel channels greatly increase the solid–liquid contact area for better convective heat transfer

03 PACKAGING

Bond it and continue packaging as usual

04 SYSTEM

The system side needs a liquid loop

MCL is direct liquid cooling. On the system side, the fan is replaced by a pump and a liquid cooling loop.

Pump
Drives the coolant around the loop
Liquid loop
Tubing and fittings carry coolant into MCL and the heat to a heat exchanger
Leak-proof design
Fittings and tubing need a leak-proof design, planned together with the integration
