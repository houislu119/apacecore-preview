HEAT PATH

Two fewer layers

Conventional cooling makes heat cross a TIM and a spreader. MCL removes both, so coolant flows right against the die.

Cross-section, schematic, not to scale

STRUCTURE

Three layers and two bonds

  1. 01

    Top wafer: manifold

    Carries the inlet and outlet portsFeeds every microchannel, then collects the flow

  2. 02

    Bottom wafer: microchannels

    Oxide-bonded (Si–Si) to the top wafer as a sealed cavityA mirrored U-path keeps flow even on both sides

  3. 03

    Die

    Bonded directly to the back of the dieHeat goes straight into the channels, with no TIM or spreader

Schematic, not to scale

NUMBERS

About one tenth the height of an air heatsink

Once bonded to the die, MCL continues through the normal packaging flow. No cold plate is bolted on afterwards.
